Features

Everything you need to check and share a part

For the workshop, the design office and 3D printing.

Section

Section planes

Section along X, Y or Z, with the slider or at a position typed in mm: cut faces are filled and hatched, so you can see inside the parts.

Measure

Measurements

Length, diameter and radius, area on hover; between two elements: minimum distance, centre distance, parallel faces, angles; point to point with snapping; wall thickness in a section.

PDF sheet

Automatic dimensions and PDF sheet

Overall size, diameters, centre distances, thicknesses, volume and surface area in one click, then a dimension sheet exported to PDF.

Exploded view

Exploded view, animation and video

Explode an assembly with its bill of materials (CSV, PDF), animate parts (rotation, translation, turntable presentation) and record the presentation as an MP4 or WebM video.

Collisions

Collisions

Analyse interferences between the parts of an assembly and move a part with stop on contact.

3D printing

Materials and 3D printing

Materials part by part, lighting and background for presentation; 3D-printing preparation (scale, orientation, splitting into segments) and STL, 3MF or OBJ export.

FAQ

Technical and sales questions

How do I activate my license after buying?
You receive your license key and the .pcvlic file by e-mail. On first launch, click "I have a license" (or, later, the Help › License… menu), then paste your key or choose the file with "Import a license file (.pcvlic)". With the installed version, double-clicking the .pcvlic file is enough. Activation works without internet.
I haven't received the license e-mail.
Check your spam folder. If nothing arrives within 24 hours, write to contact@pindiconcept.fr giving the e-mail address used for the payment.
On how many computers? What if I change PC? Updates?
The license is personal: you can use it on 2 computers that you own. Changing computers: the license can be transferred on simple request to contact@pindiconcept.fr. 1.x updates included (1.9, 1.10…); a future major version 2.0 may require a new purchase.
Can I get a refund?
As the software is delivered immediately by download, the statutory right of withdrawal does not apply when you have expressly requested immediate delivery and waived that right at payment (article L221-28, 13° of the French Consumer Code). As a goodwill gesture, we refund you on simple request within 14 days of purchase if a technical problem prevents the software from being used and we cannot solve it. Details: terms of sale.
What configuration is needed?
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with a WebGL 2 compatible graphics card. No internet connection is needed to use the software.
Is there a Mac or Linux version?
No: PindiCADViewer is available on Windows only.
Are my files sent online?
No. Your CAD files are opened and processed only on your computer. The software contains no telemetry and does not check for updates automatically. The AI assistant also runs on your PC, with no connection, and the license is checked offline, with no hardware ID. The only exception: once the license is activated, the software checks at most once a day that it has not been deactivated (after a refund or a payment dispute), sending only the license number and the version; without a connection, it works normally.
