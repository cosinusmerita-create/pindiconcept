Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Data controller

Pindi Concept — Patrick Musuyi Mupindi EI, 18 rue du Docteur Gallavardin, 69800 Saint-Priest, France. Email: contact@pindiconcept.fr.

2. Data collected during a purchase

When you make a purchase, we receive from Stripe your name, your email address, your country and the payment reference; we generate your license number. Payment data (bank card or other method) is processed only by Stripe: we never have access to it. Your license key contains your name and your email; it is stored on your computer and is shown in the software ("About" window) and in the footer of the PDF data sheets you export.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Performance of the contract: processing the order, generating and sending the license by email, support.
  • Legal obligation: accounting and retention of records.
  • Legitimate interest: fraud prevention and preventing the sharing of license keys.

4. Recipients and processors

  • Stripe: payment.
  • Cloudflare: service that generates the license after payment and keeps the license register.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France): sending the license emails.
  • Google (Gmail): support email.
  • GitHub: hosting of the website and the installers.

These providers may process data outside the European Union (in particular in the United States); these transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards (adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses).

5. Retention periods

  • Accounting records and billing data: 10 years.
  • License register (name, email, license number, payment reference): for the duration of the license, and at most 10 years.
  • Support emails: 3 years after the last exchange.

6. The software

PindiCADViewer works offline: it contains no telemetry and does not check for updates automatically. Your CAD files are processed only on your computer. The software stores on your computer your preferences, your license and a cache of models already opened, which you can clear from the File menu; this data never leaves your computer (except the license number, see below).

License check: when a license is activated, the application contacts the Pindi Concept license server at most once a day, sending only the license number and the software version (no file, no personal data), to check that the license has not been deactivated. In the event of a refund or a payment dispute, the corresponding license is deactivated. Without an internet connection, the software works normally. As with any connection, the computer's IP address reaches the server (hosted by Cloudflare); Pindi Concept does not keep it: only a non-reversible fingerprint is used, for a few minutes, to limit abuse.

New versions: at most once a day, the application reads a small public version file on the Pindi Concept website (version.json: latest version number, date, what's new). It is a simple read: nothing is sent (no license number, no file, no personal data). If a newer version exists, a banner says so; nothing is installed automatically. The free trial counters (files opened, measures, exports…) and the guided tour status stay on your computer and are never sent.

7. Website and cookies

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record technical logs (for example your IP address). The website itself sets no cookies, performs no audience measurement, uses no advertising cookies and loads no external resources (fonts, scripts, statistics). The "Buy" button takes you to the Stripe payment page, which is subject to its own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to give instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death. To exercise them, write to contact@pindiconcept.fr.

You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority): www.cnil.fr.
